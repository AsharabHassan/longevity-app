**Prepared for:** Harley Street Medical & Wellness (HSW) — Meta ads -> "biological age" quiz -> free 15-min consultation
**Research date:** 21 September 2026
**Status key:** "Verified" = seen directly on the cited page on the research date. "Not verified" = could not be confirmed from a primary source; treat as a lead, not a fact.

## 0. Method and limits (read first)

- **Meta Ad Library WAS accessible** (logged-out, via a headless browser) at `https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?active_status=active&ad_type=all&country=GB&q=<name>`. All ad copy quoted below is copied verbatim from Ad Library cards, with Library IDs so each can be re-opened at `https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=<Library ID>`.
- Limits of that method: keyword search (not page-ID search), GB, **active ads only**, first ~30-60 cards loaded per query. "No ads found" therefore means "none surfaced by keyword search", not proof that the brand runs no ads. Only ad **text** was captured; creative imagery/video was **not** reviewed, so visual-style notes come from websites and are flagged where inferred.
- Landing pages were read via fetch/headless browser. Lanserhof's London sites (`lhtac.com`, `lanserhof.com/en/london`) timed out/refused connections; thelongevitydoctor.co.uk now 301-redirects to The Private Clinic. Those rows rely on search-result snippets and press and are marked accordingly.
- Google Ads copy, Instagram/TikTok bios: **not reviewed** (not verified).
- A competitor's ad being live does not mean it is compliant. Several live ads below appear to conflict with ASA rulings and Meta's personal-attributes policy.

## 2. Dominant angles in the market

1. **"Tired / foggy / running on empty" symptom hook** — HUM2N, Get A Drip, Effect Doctors, NADclinic, Schoeman, Clinilabs, plus every supplement quiz. **Most overused**, and the one most exposed to both Meta's personal-attributes policy and the ASA's position on energy claims for IV drips.
2. **"Your GP said you're fine / normal bloods miss it"** — Effect Doctors, Clinilabs, Schoeman, Get A Drip. **Overused** and getting generic; also edges towards CAP 12.2 (don't discourage essential treatment) if pushed.
3. **Data volume as proof** — "117 health data points", "79 biomarkers", "millions of data points". **Crowded**; a numbers arms race HSW cannot win against Neko.
4. **Free short consult + scarcity** — HUM2N ("free 15-minute consultation, worth £150 … limited spots"), Harpal (10-min mini consult), Schoeman (discovery call). HSW's exact offer is already in market from HUM2N, with a value anchor HSW lacks.
5. **Discount / bundle retail** — Get A Drip codes, REVIV bundles, NADclinic 40% subscription, Clinilabs was/now price. Owns the low end; commoditises IVs.
6. **Quiet-luxury medical authority** — Lanserhof, Hooke, Neko. No offers, no symptoms; named doctors, precise language. **Under-used on Meta by London IV clinics** — this is the open lane.

Not currently contested on Meta by a credible clinic: a *doctor-built biological-age assessment*. The quiz space is held by supplement affiliates.

## 3. Differentiated positioning angles for HSW (with sample compliant headlines)

Compliance design rules applied to every headline: no named drug or drip (NAD+, methylene blue, B12), no efficacy claim for an IV, no second-person assertion of a health state, no promised age reversal, and the advertised thing is the **assessment/consultation**.

| # | Angle | Why it is credible and open | Sample headline / primary text |
|---|---|---|---|

No products sold at the end." |

## 4. Compliance red-flag list

### 4a. ASA / CAP Code (UK)

| # | Red flag | Rule / source |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Any health-benefit claim for an IV drip aimed at healthy people** — energy, immunity, anti-ageing, detox, recovery, skin/hair, mood, fat-burning. The ASA upheld three rulings on **20 December 2023**: [Get A Drip Ltd](https://www.asa.org.uk/rulings/get-a-drip-ltd-a19-549582-get-a-drip-ltd.html), [REVIV UK Ltd](https://www.asa.org.uk/rulings/reviv-uk-ltd-a19-562605-reviv-uk-ltd.html) (incl. "Detoxification", "Delivers an Energy Boost", anti-ageing), [GMG Pharmacy t/a The IV Clinic](https://www.asa.org.uk/rulings/gmg-pharmacy-ltd-a19-549495-gmg-pharmacy-ltd.html) ("anti-ageing", "detox/fat burning"). Breaches: CAP 3.1, 3.7, 12.1 (+12.23 for Get A Drip). Evidence in deficient patients "would not be relevant to advertising claims directed at generally healthy consumers." | CAP guidance [Intravenous Nutritional Therapy](https://www.asa.org.uk/advice-online/healthcare-intravenous-nutritional-therapy.html) (updated 6 Feb 2024): "The ASA has yet to see convincing evidence to support the claimed benefits of specific IVNT's." |
| 2 | **Sanction risk is real:** Get A Drip was placed on the ASA's [non-compliant advertisers list](https://www.asa.org.uk/non-compliant/get-a-drip-ltd.html) on 1 Feb 2024 and the page was still live on 21 Sep 2026. | ASA |
| 3 | **Drip names that are themselves claims** ("Immunity", "Energy", "Detox", "Anti-ageing" drip) count as implied claims — the Dec 2023 rulings covered product names such as "IMMUNO-BOOST Immunity Support". | Rulings above |
| 4 | **Prescription-only medicines must not be advertised to the public** — CAP **12.12**: "Prescription-only medicines or prescription-only medical treatments may not be advertised to the public." In paid ads and social, "almost every reference to a POM" (named or not, including hashtags, price offers and discount codes) is treated as promotion. Advertise "a consultation for [condition/concern]" instead. | [POM guidance](https://www.asa.org.uk/advice-online/healthcare-prescription-only-medicine.html) (17 Feb 2022); [Code section 12](https://www.asa.org.uk/type/non_broadcast/code_section/12.html) |
| 5 | **HSW-specific POM exposure:** (a) **Methylene blue IV** — methylthioninium chloride injection is a licensed POM (indicated for methaemoglobinaemia) ([emc SmPC](https://www.medicines.org.uk/emc/product/6898/smpc)); naming it in an ad is a 12.12 issue, and promoting it for longevity is off-label. (b) **Injectable B12 and vitamin D** are POMs — ASA [enforcement notice, 28 May 2020](https://www.asa.org.uk/news/enforcing-the-rules-for-prescription-only-medicines-and-vitamin-shots.html); relevant to Myers-type drips containing B12. (c) **NAD+ IV** holds no UK marketing authorisation (stated by secondary source [Bolt Pharmacy](https://www.boltpharmacy.co.uk/guide/what-are-nad-injections); MHRA position **not verified** here). CAP 12.11 ("Medicines must have a licence … before they are marketed") and 12.1 bite on any medicinal claim. **No ASA ruling specifically on NAD+ IV was found** — absence of a ruling is not clearance. (d) Metabolic programme: if it involves GLP-1s, see ASA [weight-loss POM enforcement](https://www.asa.org.uk/advice-online/weight-control-prescription-only-medicines.html). |
| 6 | **Website:** homepage must sell the consultation, with no direct or indirect POM references (logos, testimonials, hover text); POM info only on inner pages, "balanced and factual", as a possible outcome of consultation; no POM price lists on the homepage and no price-led encouragement. "NAD+ IV £295" as homepage/ad hero copy should be reviewed with CAP Copy Advice. | [POMs on websites](https://www.asa.org.uk/advice-online/health-prescription-only-medicines-websites.html) |
| 7 | **"Detox"** — "the body's liver and kidneys automatically detoxify"; ASA has not seen evidence toxins can be removed by other means. Applies squarely to EBOO/ozone and phospholipid-exchange copy. | [Detoxing: General](https://www.asa.org.uk/advice-online/detoxing-general.html) (25 Oct 2022) |
| 8 | **"Biological age" / "X years younger"** — ASA upheld against [Beiersdorf (Eucerin), 29 April 2026](https://www.asa.org.uk/rulings/beiersdorf-uk-ltd-a25-1317807-beiersdorf-uk-ltd-.html): "look up to 5 years younger … clinically proven" was unsubstantiated; self-reported data was not robust. For HSW: a questionnaire score must not be presented as a measured biological age, as "clinically proven", or as something treatment will reduce by N years. Label it an estimate/indicator and say what it is based on. |
| 9 | **Doctors and celebrities must not endorse medicines** (12.18); no before/after for POMs. A doctor may front the clinic and the assessment, not the drug. | Code s.12 |
| 10 | **Don't discourage essential treatment** (12.2) and don't offer diagnosis at a distance for medicinal products (12.2.1) — the quiz result must not read as a diagnosis; "your GP missed it" copy needs care. | Code s.12 |
| 11 | **Disease claims** (12.6) and COVID/viral claims — ASA [enforcement notice 27 Apr 2020](https://www.asa.org.uk/news/putting-iv-drip-advertisers-on-notice.html); 2020 rulings included REVIV and The Private Harley Street Clinic. | ASA |
| 12 | **Savings / "worth £X" / urgency** ("limited spots") must be genuine and provable (CAP section 3 pricing and availability rules) — not verified against a specific ruling in this session. |

**What the ASA will allow:** advertising a doctor consultation or health assessment; naming concerns/conditions a consultation addresses; factual descriptions of process, credentials (GMC, CQC), location, price of the consultation; lifestyle and measurement language without efficacy claims.

### 4c. Quiz-specific checklist

- Call it an "indicator"/"estimate" from a lifestyle questionnaire; state it is not a laboratory measurement or diagnosis (CAP 3.1/3.7; Beiersdorf ruling).
- Results page must not recommend a named POM or drip; it recommends a doctor conversation (CAP 12.12, 12.2.1).
- No "you are ageing faster than X%" without a documented, defensible basis.
- Neutral result URLs and event names; standard events only; health answers stay server-side (Meta prohibited-information terms).
- Ad copy in third person / service-descriptive; no age or symptom call-outs to the reader (Meta personal attributes).
- Explicit consent for health data at quiz start; marketing consent separate (UK GDPR — not researched in depth here).
- Get CAP Copy Advice (free, ~24h) on the hero headline and results screen before launch.

## 5. Observed non-compliance in competitor ads (do not copy)

- Get A Drip's live ad claims drips "support energy, hydration and recovery" and Vitamin C gives "your immune system and skin some extra support" — the claim types upheld against it in Dec 2023, while it remains on the ASA non-compliant list.
- NADclinic's pen ads claim "sustained focus, cognitive resilience", "supports cellular repair".
- HUM2N, Effect Doctors, Clinilabs use second-person symptom/age questions that match Meta's own disallowed examples.
- Harpal Clinic's ad names testosterone formulations.

These running does not indicate safety; ASA enforcement is complaint-led and Meta review is inconsistent. REVIV's softened 2026 copy ("support your wellness routine", consultation-first) shows what a post-ruling advertiser looks like.
