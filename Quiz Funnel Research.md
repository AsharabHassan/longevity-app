## 1. QUIZ FUNNEL BEST PRACTICES & CONVERSION DATA

### Optimal Question Count

| Quiz Length | Completion Rate | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| Short (3-7 questions) | 65-85% | Quick lead capture, broad audiences |
| Medium (8-15 questions) | 45-65% | Health assessments, treatment matching |
| Long (16+ questions) | 25-45% | Biological age tests, deep segmentation |

**Key finding:** 7 questions is where engagement peaks before declining. However, for health/wellness topics specifically, users will complete longer quizzes (15-30 questions) because they genuinely want accurate results. One practitioner maintained a 50% completion rate with 35 questions.

**Recommended sweet spot for a longevity/IV clinic:** 12-18 questions. Health & wellness quizzes specifically benchmark at 50-65% completion. The perceived value of the result (biological age, wellness score, personalized treatment plan) justifies the length.

### Conversion Rate Benchmarks

- **Overall quiz-to-lead conversion:** 40.1% average (people who start the quiz become a lead)
- **Quiz opt-in rate:** Up to 55% (25x better than pop-ups or standard lead forms)
- **Quiz traffic vs. regular traffic:** Blume (wellness brand) saw 22% conversion on quiz traffic vs. 2% on regular site traffic
- **Quiz funnels vs. standard landing pages:** 30%+ conversion vs. 2-3% industry average
- **Wellness center case study:** 1,600+ leads generated through a pain quiz funnel, leading to hundreds of new patients

### Completion Rate by Quiz Type

- **Personality quizzes:** 60-80% (highest engagement)
- **Assessment quizzes:** 45-65% (ideal for health scoring)
- **Product recommendation quizzes:** 40-60% (lower completion but higher-quality leads)

### Completion Rate by Traffic Source

| Source | Completion Rate |
|---|---|
| Email subscribers | 70-85% |
| Google search | 55-70% |
| Social media | 45-60% |
| Paid ads | 35-50% |

## 2. QUIZ FUNNEL CONVERSION OPTIMIZATION

### Progress Bars
- Progress bars increase completion rates by **12-18%**
- Non-linear progress bars (that jump ahead unpredictably) perform even better -- they create positive surprises
- Visual progress reduces anxiety and drop-off by setting clear expectations

### Question Format Best Practices

**Use multiple choice / image-select over text input:**
- Multiple-choice buttons and image selects keep the experience interactive and fast
- Quizzes with images convert **43% more leads** than text-only versions
- Sliders work well for scale-based questions (energy levels, pain, sleep quality) but should be used sparingly
- Avoid open-ended text boxes entirely -- they kill momentum and completion rates

**Recommended format mix for a wellness quiz:**
- 70% multiple choice with icons/images (e.g., "What best describes your energy levels?")
- 15% visual scale/slider (e.g., "Rate your sleep quality 1-10")
- 10% image-selection cards (e.g., "Which of these concerns you most?" with visual cards)
- 5% simple numeric input (e.g., age, hours of sleep)

### Mobile Optimization
- **60% of quiz traffic comes from mobile devices**
- Each additional second of load time costs **7% completion rate**
- One question per screen on mobile
- Large tap targets for answer options
- Thumb-friendly button placement

### Psychology of Commitment (Escalation Ladder)
1. **Micro-commitments:** Each question answered creates momentum -- users invest effort and don't want to abandon
2. **Consistency principle:** After answering a few easy questions, giving contact info feels like a small next step
3. **Curiosity gap:** Users become invested in seeing their results/score
4. **Sunk cost:** The more questions answered, the more reluctant users are to quit

### Lead Capture Timing
- Place the email/contact form **between the last question and the results** -- this is when emotional engagement peaks
- Frame it as: "Enter your email to get your personalized results + treatment recommendations"
- Never place the lead form at the start or mid-quiz -- it kills momentum

### Question Sequencing Strategy
1. **Start easy:** Demographics, simple lifestyle questions (age, primary health goal)
2. **Build engagement:** Interesting, personally relevant questions (energy, sleep, symptoms)
3. **Deepen commitment:** More specific health questions
4. **Sensitive questions last:** Budget, contact info -- after maximum commitment investment

### Pre-Framing Techniques for Sensitive Questions
- **Value-first framing:** "To give you the most accurate score, we need to know..."
- **Social proof framing:** "Over 10,000 people have discovered their biological age..."
- **Future-pacing:** "Your answers will help us create a personalized longevity plan..."

## 4. QUESTION CATEGORIES MAPPED TO SPECIFIC TREATMENTS

### EBOO Therapy (Ozone Blood Treatment)
**Trigger symptoms/indicators to ask about:**
- Chronic fatigue or persistent low energy
- Frequent infections or slow wound healing
- History of Lyme disease, chronic viral infections, or autoimmune conditions
- Circulatory issues (cold hands/feet, poor circulation)
- Chronic inflammatory conditions (joint pain, arthritis)
- Environmental toxin exposure (mold, heavy metals, chemicals)
- Feeling "toxic" -- headaches, brain fog, malaise
- Cardiovascular concerns
- Skin conditions that won't resolve

**Screening exclusions:** G6PD deficiency, pregnancy, bleeding disorders, heparin intolerance, uncontrolled hyperthyroidism, recent MI

### NAD+ IV Drip
**Trigger symptoms/indicators:**
- Low energy / chronic fatigue
- Brain fog / declining cognitive sharpness
- Feeling "older than your age" / accelerated aging concerns
- Poor recovery from physical exertion
- History of substance use / addiction recovery
- Mood disturbances (depression, anxiety)
- Interest in anti-aging / cellular repair
- Poor sleep quality affecting daytime function
- High-demand professionals seeking cognitive edge
- Athletes wanting faster recovery

**Screening exclusions:** Pregnancy, family/recent history of cancer

### Detox IV Drip
**Trigger symptoms/indicators:**
- Hangover recovery (recent alcohol consumption)
- Headaches, nausea, general malaise
- Feeling sluggish after poor dietary choices
- Environmental toxin exposure
- Liver health concerns
- Bloating / digestive discomfort
- Skin breakouts
- Travel recovery
- Post-medication detox support
- "Reset" after holiday / party season

### Immunity IV Drip
**Trigger symptoms/indicators:**
- Frequent colds, flu, or infections (3+ per year)
- Slow recovery from illness
- Upcoming travel (especially air travel)
- High-stress periods compromising immunity
- Seasonal illness prevention
- Post-exposure to sick individuals
- Feeling "run down" but not yet sick
- Chronic low-grade immune issues
- Nutrient absorption problems
- Pre/post surgery immune support

### Skin Glow IV Drip
**Trigger symptoms/indicators:**
- Dull, tired-looking skin
- Premature aging signs (fine lines, loss of elasticity)
- Uneven skin tone / hyperpigmentation
- Acne or chronic skin issues
- Desire for "glow from within" before events
- Hair and nail brittleness
- Sun damage concerns
- Collagen production decline (age 30+)
- Poor response to topical skincare
- Preparing for a special occasion

### Myers Cocktail
**Trigger symptoms/indicators:**
- General fatigue and low energy
- Known or suspected vitamin deficiencies
- Chronic fatigue syndrome
- Fibromyalgia
- Frequent migraines or headaches
- Muscle cramps or spasms
- Seasonal allergies / chronic sinusitis
- Asthma
- General "wellness maintenance"
- Stress-related exhaustion
- Poor dietary habits / nutrient gaps

### Phospholipid Exchange IV
**Trigger symptoms/indicators:**
- Neurological symptoms (tingling, numbness, cognitive decline)
- History of neurodegenerative concerns
- Chronic fatigue with no clear cause
- Toxic exposure history (mold, heavy metals, pesticides)
- Liver dysfunction / high cholesterol
- Autoimmune conditions
- Chronic Lyme disease
- Cell membrane damage concerns
- Poor response to other treatments
- Cardiovascular disease risk

### IV Methylene Blue
**Trigger symptoms/indicators:**
- Brain fog / cognitive decline
- Memory issues / difficulty with recall
- Low mental energy despite adequate sleep
- Interest in cognitive enhancement / nootropics
- Neurodegenerative disease risk (family history)
- Mitochondrial dysfunction symptoms
- Chronic fatigue with cognitive component
- Students/professionals seeking peak mental performance
- Post-viral cognitive symptoms
- Age-related cognitive concerns

**Screening exclusion:** Must screen for SSRI/SNRI/MAOI use (serotonin syndrome risk)

### Metabolic Health Programme
**Trigger symptoms/indicators:**
- Weight gain resistant to diet/exercise
- Blood sugar instability (energy crashes, cravings)
- Family history of type 2 diabetes
- High waist circumference
- Known insulin resistance or metabolic syndrome
- Fatigue after meals (postprandial somnolence)
- High triglycerides / low HDL
- High blood pressure
- PCOS or hormonal imbalances linked to metabolism
- Desire to optimize body composition

## 5. BIOLOGICAL AGE ASSESSMENT QUESTIONS

### Validated/Semi-Validated Frameworks

**NOVOS ENABL Age (University of Washington):**
- A second-generation, survey-based biological age clock
- Trained using XAI (Explainable Artificial Intelligence) on tens of thousands of people
- Claims greater accuracy than first-generation blood-based epigenetic clocks
- Uses questionnaire responses about lifestyle and health behaviors
- Produces a score out of 100 reflecting longevity alignment

**Sharecare RealAge Test:**
- Clinically validated, based on 25,000 medical studies
- Identifies 125 factors that influence aging rate
- Takes 15-20 minutes
- **13 health factors measured:** stress, activity, sleep, relationships, weight, blood pressure, blood glucose, cholesterol, smoking, drinking, diet, medications, fitness

**Longevies Biological Age Calculator:**
- 6 categories: Basic Information, Lifestyle & Habits, Physical Activity & Fitness, Nutrition & Diet, Environmental & Additional Factors, Mental & Emotional Well-being

### Recommended Biological Age Questions for Your Quiz

**Category 1: Sleep Quality (High impact on biological aging)**
- How many hours of sleep do you typically get per night? (Under 5 / 5-6 / 6-7 / 7-8 / 8-9 / Over 9)
- How would you rate your sleep quality? (Poor - I wake frequently / Fair / Good / Excellent - I wake refreshed)
- Do you have trouble falling asleep or staying asleep? (Rarely / Sometimes / Often / Almost always)

**Category 2: Physical Activity**
- How many days per week do you exercise for 30+ minutes? (0 / 1-2 / 3-4 / 5-6 / Daily)
- What type of exercise do you primarily do? (None / Walking only / Cardio / Strength training / Mix of cardio + strength / High-intensity training)
- How sedentary is your typical day? (Mostly sitting / Some movement / Moderately active / Very active)

**Category 3: Nutrition & Diet**
- How many servings of fruits and vegetables do you eat daily? (0-1 / 2-3 / 4-5 / 6+)
- How often do you eat processed/fast food? (Daily / Several times per week / Occasionally / Rarely/never)
- How much water do you drink daily? (Less than 4 glasses / 4-6 / 6-8 / 8+)
- How often do you consume alcohol? (Never / Occasionally / Weekly / Daily)

**Category 4: Stress & Mental Health**
- How would you rate your daily stress level? (Low / Moderate / High / Very high)
- Do you practice any stress management? (Meditation/mindfulness / Exercise / Therapy / Nothing specific)
- How would you rate your overall mood/mental well-being? (Excellent / Good / Fair / Poor)

**Category 5: Social & Relationships**
- Do you feel you have strong social connections? (Yes / Somewhat / Not really / No)
- How often do you feel lonely or isolated? (Rarely / Sometimes / Often / Very often)

**Category 6: Health Markers (self-reported)**
- Do you smoke or vape? (Never / Former / Occasionally / Daily)
- Do you know your blood pressure status? (Normal / Slightly high / High / Don't know)
- Do you have any chronic health conditions? (None / 1 / 2-3 / 4+)
- BMI range or waist circumference category

**Category 7: Recovery & Vitality**
- How quickly do you recover from physical exertion? (Quickly / Within a day / Takes several days / Very slowly)
- How would you rate your energy levels throughout the day? (High and stable / Good with some dips / Low energy, need stimulants / Chronically fatigued)

## 6. WELLNESS SCORE METHODOLOGY

### How to Build a Credible Wellness/Cellular Health Score

#### A. Scoring Architecture

**Recommended approach: Weighted multi-dimensional score on a 0-100 scale**

| Dimension | Weight | What It Measures |
|---|---|---|
| Energy & Vitality | 15% | Energy levels, fatigue, recovery speed |
| Sleep Quality | 15% | Duration, quality, consistency |
| Cognitive Function | 12% | Brain fog, memory, focus, clarity |
| Immune Resilience | 10% | Frequency of illness, recovery speed |
| Metabolic Health | 12% | Diet quality, blood sugar stability, weight management |
| Physical Activity | 12% | Exercise frequency, type, consistency |
| Stress & Mental Wellness | 12% | Stress levels, mood, coping mechanisms |
| Cellular & Skin Health | 7% | Skin quality, aging signs, inflammation |
| Social & Purpose | 5% | Relationships, sense of purpose, loneliness |

#### B. Scoring Methodology

**Step 1: Score each question 0-4 points**
- Each answer option maps to a score (best answer = 4, worst = 0)

**Step 2: Calculate dimension scores**
- Average the question scores within each dimension
- Normalize to 0-100 for each dimension

**Step 3: Apply weights to get composite score**
- Multiply each dimension score by its weight
- Sum for total Wellness Score (0-100)

**Step 4: Generate biological age estimate**
- Use the composite score to estimate biological age offset from chronological age
- Score 85-100: Biological age 5-10 years younger
- Score 70-84: Biological age roughly equal to chronological age
- Score 55-69: Biological age 3-7 years older
- Score below 55: Biological age 8-15 years older

#### C. Score Ranges & Labels

| Score | Label | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 90-100 | Optimal | Exceptional cellular health; aging well below your chronological age |
| 80-89 | Strong | Good health foundation; minor areas for optimization |
| 70-79 | Average | Room for meaningful improvement in key areas |
| 60-69 | Below Average | Multiple areas need attention; intervention recommended |
| Below 60 | Needs Attention | Significant health risks; comprehensive programme recommended |

#### D. Credibility Factors

- Reference the RealAge methodology (25,000 studies, 125 factors)
- Reference NOVOS ENABL Age (University of Washington, XAI-trained)
- Use language like "based on peer-reviewed longevity research" or "informed by validated biological age assessment methodologies"
- Wild Health model: 32 health markers converted to hazard ratios, score range 60-100
- Include a disclaimer: "This is an indicative wellness assessment, not a medical diagnosis"

#### E. Treatment Mapping from Score

Each dimension score naturally maps to treatment recommendations:

| Low-Scoring Dimension | Primary Treatment Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Energy & Vitality | NAD+ IV, Myers Cocktail |
| Sleep Quality | NAD+ IV, Metabolic Health Programme |
| Cognitive Function | IV Methylene Blue, NAD+ IV, Phospholipid Exchange |
| Immune Resilience | Immunity IV Drip, Myers Cocktail |
| Metabolic Health | Metabolic Health Programme, Detox IV |
| Cellular & Skin Health | Skin Glow IV, EBOO Therapy |
| Stress & Mental Wellness | NAD+ IV, Myers Cocktail |
| Overall low score / chronic issues | EBOO Therapy, Phospholipid Exchange |
